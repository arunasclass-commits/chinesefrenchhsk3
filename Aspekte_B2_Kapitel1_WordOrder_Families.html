Why German word order feels different. English keeps the verb next to its helper ("have emigrated"). German splits them: the finite verb sits in position 2, and the second verb part (participle / infinitive / separable prefix) jumps to the very end. Those two form a bracket — the Satzklammer — that wraps the whole sentence. The middle follows te‑ka‑mo‑lo: time → reason → manner → place. Drop each colored block into its slot and you'll feel it.
